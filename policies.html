Store Policies

Based in Oakville, Ontario, Cricket Hub Canada proudly serves customers across the Greater Toronto Area (GTA), with shipping available throughout Canada.

← Back to Home

Local Delivery, Shipping & Oakville Pickup

  • Most products are sourced after availability is confirmed and are normally ready within approximately 5–7 business days.
  • Local delivery is available across Oakville and eligible GTA locations after confirmation. Any applicable delivery charge will be confirmed before payment.
  • Free Oakville pickup is available by appointment once your order is ready. The exact pickup location will be shared privately.
  • Canada-wide shipping is available for eligible products after the destination, package size and courier charges are confirmed.
  • Shipping estimates begin after the product has been received or released by our supplier.
  • If an item becomes unavailable before payment, we will offer an alternative or allow you to cancel your request.

Returns & Product Concerns

  • Please contact us before returning any item. Return authorization and instructions are required.
  • Unused accessories in original condition may be considered for return within 7 days of receipt, subject to supplier approval and return shipping costs.
  • Used, knocked-in, oiled, damaged or custom-selected cricket bats are generally considered final sale unless a verified manufacturing defect exists.
  • Natural differences in willow grain, shade, weight and minor cosmetic marks are normal characteristics and are not considered manufacturing defects.
  • Please report shipping damage within 24 hours of delivery and retain all packaging. Include clear photographs of both the parcel and the product.
  • Warranty claims are reviewed with the supplier or manufacturer and are subject to their approval.

Privacy Notice

When you submit a website inquiry, we collect the information you provide, including your name, email address, phone number, city, selected product and message. We use this information solely to respond to your inquiry, confirm product availability and assist with your order.

  • Inquiry submissions may be securely stored in a private Google Sheet and emailed directly to the business owner.
  • We never sell or share your personal information for marketing purposes.
  • Inquiry information is retained only as reasonably necessary for customer service, order records and legal obligations.
  • You may request correction or deletion of your inquiry information where permitted by law.

Website & Ordering Terms

  • All prices are displayed in Canadian Dollars (CAD) and are subject to change before an order is confirmed.
  • This website serves as an inquiry catalogue and does not provide live inventory or online checkout.
  • Orders are confirmed only after product availability, pricing, taxes (if applicable), shipping charges and payment details have been agreed upon.
  • Product photographs may be representative. Exact bat photographs, grains and weights can be provided whenever available.
  • Product descriptions are prepared in good faith using supplier information. Please contact us if a specific specification is essential before ordering.
  • Cricket Hub Canada reserves the right to correct pricing, product descriptions or availability before accepting payment.
